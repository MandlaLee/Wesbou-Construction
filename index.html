03 / How we work

Local crew. Direct answers. No vanishing act.

Wesbou Construction is a Phalaborwa-based contractor working across the Mopani district. We are not a Johannesburg company with a satellite office — the person who quotes your job is the same person you can phone when the site is open.

That matters here. Phalaborwa’s ground conditions, summer heat, and the pace of mining-linked projects do not reward contractors who learn on the job. We price for the work we can actually staff and finish, and we say so when a scope is outside what we do well.

If you need a straight answer on a build, a renovation, or a concrete structure, ask for a site visit. We will walk the property, point out what we see, and give you a written scope before any work starts.

  1. Walk the site

    We meet you on the property, check access, levels, and existing services, and flag anything that will change the cost before you commit.

  2. Written scope & quote

    You get a line-item breakdown: what is included, what is excluded, and when each phase will happen. No lump-sum guesswork.

  3. Build in phases

    We sequence the work around weather, material lead times, and your operating hours. You get a single point of contact on site.

  4. Handover & snag list

    Before we call a job done, we walk it with you, list the snags, and close them out. The final payment follows your sign-off, not ours.
